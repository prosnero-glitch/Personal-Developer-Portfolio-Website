About

Emekpa Prosper · Frontend Developer

I’m Emekpa Prosper, a frontend developer. I build the part of a website people actually see and use: layouts, navigation, forms, and everything that has to work on a phone as well as it does on a laptop.

I started with one HTML page that looked terrible. I kept fixing it, and somewhere along the way fixing things became the part I enjoy most. Since then I’ve moved from following tutorials to building my own projects, from a recipe finder that uses a public API to a typing test that keeps score as you type.

Right now I’m taking an advanced frontend course, going deeper into JavaScript, responsive design and deployment. I’m looking for an internship or junior role where I can learn from a team and ship real work. I’m also open to freelance projects.
